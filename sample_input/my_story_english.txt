There was once a boy who walked through the gates of his college with a suitcase in one hand, dreams in his eyes, and a thousand unanswered questions in his heart.

He never wanted to stay in the hostel.

The room felt strange. The faces were unfamiliar. The food wasn't like home. The nights felt longer than they ever had. Every evening he would count the days left before he could go back to his family. Home was all he could think about.

In those first few weeks, he kept telling himself,

*"Just a little longer... then I'll go home."*

But life has a beautiful habit of changing us when we least expect it.

Days quietly turned into weeks.

Weeks became semesters.

And without making any announcement, something extraordinary happened.

The strangers sitting beside him in the mess became brothers.

The room he once disliked slowly filled with stories.

The corridors that once felt empty began echoing with laughter, midnight birthday celebrations, last-minute assignment panic, chai breaks, endless conversations, cricket matches, emotional breakdowns, silly fights, patch-ups, and dreams whispered under the stars.

The hostel stopped feeling like a building.

It became a feeling.

It became home.

Somewhere along the journey, he stopped counting the days left to leave.

In fact...

He fell so deeply in love with this life that there came a time when he didn't go back home for three straight years.

Not because he had forgotten his family.

But because, unknowingly, he had found another one.

The hostel had become more than just a place to sleep.

It had become the place where he truly learned how to live.

I watched him grow.

I saw him arrive as a shy, confused boy who barely knew anyone.

I saw him fail.

I saw him question himself.

I saw him sit silently after disappointing days.

I saw him celebrate victories that once felt impossible.

I saw him learn that success isn't about never falling—it's about finding the courage to stand up one more time.

Slowly, the boy who once hesitated to introduce himself became someone people looked up to.

His confidence wasn't built inside classrooms alone.

It was built in hostel rooms at 2 AM.

In sleepless nights before exams.

In countless cups of tea.

In shared meals when money was short.

In friendships that needed no words.

In the shoulders that carried each other through the hardest days.

The hostel didn't just give him memories.

It gave him a family.

It gave him an identity.

It gave him a version of himself he never knew existed.

And then...

Almost without warning...

The final day arrived.

Once again, he stood before the same college gate through which he had entered years ago.

Only this time, everything was different.

The gate hadn't changed.

The buildings hadn't changed.

The hostel still stood exactly where it always had.

But he had.

His footsteps were slower now.

His heart was heavier.

Every corner carried a memory.

Every staircase reminded him of laughter.

Every room held pieces of conversations that would never happen again.

He looked around one last time, silently trying to memorize everything.

The walls.

The corridors.

The balcony.

The mess.

The late-night tea spot.

The people.

The chaos.

The life.

Because deep inside, he knew...

This wasn't just a place he was leaving.

He was saying goodbye to the most beautiful chapter of his life.

And if those hostel walls could speak, perhaps they would gently smile and say—

*"You were never meant to stay here forever."*

*"You came here as a boy searching for a future."*

*"You leave as a man carrying stories, friendships, lessons, scars, dreams, and a heart much bigger than the one you arrived with."*

*"Every sleepless night, every failure, every success, every laugh, every tear, every goodbye... was preparing you for the life waiting beyond these gates."*

The world outside will not be as forgiving.

It will be faster.

Harder.

More uncertain.

There will be days when no one will knock on his door asking,

*"Bhai, chai pe chale?"*

No spontaneous midnight walks.

No noisy corridors.

No birthdays celebrated with cake smashed across faces.

No hostel rooftop conversations about dreams.

Life will move on.

Everyone will.

But somewhere, hidden inside him, this hostel will continue to exist.

Years later...

When he hears someone laugh in a corridor...

When the smell of Maggi fills a room...

When an old photograph appears on his phone...

Or when life becomes too heavy...

He will close his eyes and return here, if only for a moment.

To the place where strangers became family.

Where ordinary days became unforgettable memories.

Where a boy discovered who he truly was.

So today...

I don't want to say goodbye.

Because some places deserve something better than goodbye.

Instead, I simply smile and whisper—

**All the best for your corporate journey and for the beautiful life waiting ahead. Chase your dreams with the same courage that brought you here. Stay humble in success, stay strong in failure, and never lose the kindness this place taught you.**

And whenever life feels overwhelming...

Remember this.

There was once a boy who entered these gates carrying nothing but dreams.

He left carrying friendships that time could never erase...

Memories that distance could never fade...

And a home that existed not in bricks and walls—

but in people, moments, and a piece of his heart that would forever remain inside those hostel corridors.

Because people may leave the hostel...

But the hostel never really leaves them.
